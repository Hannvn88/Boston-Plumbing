# Brag Plan: Boston Plumbing — the AI Voice Agent

## What is this app?
A premium white "emergency plumbing" website whose real product is a working, real-time **AI voice assistant**: press one button, speak a question, and a glassy emerald orb reacts live to your voice while a self-hosted pipeline (Groq Whisper → LLaMA → Piper TTS, orchestrated in n8n) transcribes, reasons, and speaks the answer back. Built by Wayforé Studio as a portfolio demo — the plumbing theme is a wrapper; the flex is the voice agent.

## The angle
**The plumbing site is a Trojan horse.** It looks like a tidy local-business site — but the centerpiece isn't pipes, it's a voice AI you can actually talk to, running in real time, on standard web. The video leads with the living orb and the real interaction (press → speak → orb reacts → it answers out loud), then pulls back the curtain on the self-hosted stack, then lands the pitch: *this can live inside any business's site.* Specific, not generic: real orb, real one-button flow, real status copy, real stack names.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The emerald **glass voice orb** blooms onto a clean white screen, breathing/glowing, with the product's own line: **"Talk to us. We're listening."** A living object + a promise — no logo card, no plumbing. It signals "this thing is alive" instantly.

## Key moments (the middle)
- **Press & speak.** A forest-green mic button; a cursor taps it (ripple + soft UI tick). The orb shifts to *listening* — its surface warps into a live waveform. A transcript types in: *"Do you handle burst pipes at 2am?"* (the plumbing wrapper enters naturally, as the demo question).
- **Think → answer.** Orb spins restlessly (*thinking*, three dots), then pulses rhythmically (*speaking*). The answer card fades in with the site's real forest-green left border: *"Yes — licensed plumbers on call 24/7, usually at your door within 45 minutes."* Status line: *"Here's your answer…"*
- **Under the hood.** Three clean cards arrive one-by-one on the beat: **Hears you — Whisper** · **Thinks — LLaMA** · **Speaks back — Piper**, subtitle *"orchestrated in n8n · self-hosted."* This is the capability flex.

## Outro / punchline
Line: **"It's running inside a plumbing site. It could run inside yours."** → Wayforé Studio wordmark + **"Web + AI automation."** Clean, confident, no wink needed.

## User flow worth showing
The working app's core loop (entry → key action → result):
1. **Entry:** press the mic button.
2. **Key action:** speak; the orb reacts live to your voice (listening → thinking → speaking).
3. **Result:** the answer fades in on screen and is spoken aloud.
This loop is the centerpiece (Scenes 1–3). The stack + pitch (Scenes 4–5) frame it.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, professional business bed with clean motion-matched UI accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` (bundled), ~120 BPM — upbeat but restrained, agency-showreel energy
- Music treatment: start ~0s at a moderate bed level; small lift into the stack reveal (~13s); gentle fade-out over the outro tail. Let strong beats (16–20s) carry the pipeline cards + Wayforé slam.
- Music cue guidance: preset read (120.19 BPM). Strong cues in window: **17.02s, 17.52s, 18.52s, 20.02s**. Use the beat grid ~13.0–17.5s (every ~0.5s) for the 3 stack cards — but reveal on *every other* beat (~1.0s apart) so each card holds long enough to read; land the Wayforé outro on the 20.02s strong cue.
- Audio-reactive treatment: **subtle** — orb glow/scale may breathe with music RMS/bass during the hook and the "speaking" beat; no literal waveform bars beyond the diegetic listening warp.
- SFX posture: **sparse, professional, motion-matched** — one soft UI tick on the mic tap; light key ticks under the transcript type; a soft pop/chime per stack card; a gentle confirm as the answer card settles. No casino/impact bombast.
- Audio-coupled moments: mic tap (tick), transcript typing (key ticks), 3 stack cards (pop per card, beat-aligned), answer card settle (soft confirm), Wayforé slam (on strong beat).
- Restraint rule: audio must stay premium and quiet-confident; never carnival. If a cue doesn't match real on-screen motion, drop it.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The orb wakes — 3.5s
White screen, faint grain. The emerald **glass orb** scales/fades in center and settles into a slow breathing glow (soft green halo behind it). Display line fades up beneath it: **"Talk to us. We're listening."** Hold long enough to read.
Sequential/interaction: none (single hero reveal).
Audio intent: music opens; a soft ascending presence as the orb blooms; establishes calm confidence.
Audio-coupled idea: subtle orb glow breathes with the bed; no SFX spike.
Music: warm bed in.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Press & speak — 4.5s
The orb moves up slightly; a **forest-green circular mic button** sits below with a small status line. A cursor glides in and **taps** it (press ripple). Orb switches to **listening**: its surface warps into a live, voice-reactive waveform. Status: *"We're listening."* A transcript **types in** below: *"Do you handle burst pipes at 2am?"*
Sequential/interaction: **yes** — simulate a cursor tap on the mic button; then type the transcript character-by-character.
Audio intent: crisp, tactile — the tap and typing make it feel real and responsive.
Audio-coupled idea: soft UI tick on tap; light key ticks under the typing.
Music: bed continues, unobtrusive.
Transition mood: clean → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Think, then answer — 4.5s
Orb spins restlessly (**thinking**) with three pulsing dots and status *"Finding your answer…"*, then shifts to **speaking** — rhythmic pulse + brightening core. The **answer card** fades in with the site's real forest-green left border: *"Yes — licensed plumbers on call 24/7, usually at your door within 45 minutes."* Status: *"Here's your answer…"*
Sequential/interaction: **yes** — dots → answer card reveal, in sequence; card holds ~1.6s to read.
Audio intent: a brief "working" lull, then a warm resolve as the answer lands.
Audio-coupled idea: soft confirm/chime as the answer card settles; orb pulse loosely tracks the bed.
Music: small dip during thinking, back up on the answer.
Transition mood: soft → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Under the hood — 4.5s
Clean white feature-card row. Three cards arrive **one by one on the beat**, each an emerald dot + label: **"Hears you — Whisper (Groq)"** · **"Thinks — LLaMA"** · **"Speaks back — Piper"**. Subtitle settles under the set: *"orchestrated in n8n · self-hosted · real-time."*
Sequential/interaction: **yes** — 3 cards reveal ~1.0s apart (every other beat, ~13.0/14.0/15.0s), then the full set holds ~1.5s with the subtitle.
Audio intent: momentum builds; each card lands on a beat like a checklist completing.
Audio-coupled idea: soft pop/chime per card, beat-aligned; subtle music lift.
Music: lift into the strong-beat window.
Transition mood: smooth wipe → Scene 5

### Scene 5 — It could run inside yours — 3.5s
Line settles center: **"It's running inside a plumbing site. It could run inside yours."** Then a clean cut/slam on the **20.02s strong beat** to the **Wayforé Studio** wordmark + tagline **"Web + AI automation."** The small emerald orb reprises beside/above the wordmark as a signature. Hold on the outro.
Sequential/interaction: pitch line → Wayforé wordmark reveal.
Audio intent: confident resolve; the wordmark lands on the strong beat, then the bed fades out clean.
Audio-coupled idea: single soft confirm as the wordmark lands; no bombast.
Music: slam on 20.02s strong cue, then gentle fade to silence on the tail.
Transition mood: clean slam → hold
